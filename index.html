The agent age

AI is about to know everything about us.

Soon each of us will have agents working on our behalf, booking, writing, remembering and deciding. They only work well when they know us: our files, our habits, our clients, our children.

The easy place to run all of that is a big platform's cloud, and the price is quiet but enormous: every agent's memory, every app's records and every question we ask becomes data held, and often used, by someone else.

Edgible lets your apps and agents run where your data lives, on machines you control. You shouldn't have to choose between capable AI and control of your own life.

How it works

Three parts, so you never think about servers.

At home, in the office or on a small cloud server, it works the same way.

A safe front door

A small agent on your machine connects out to Edgible, so each app gets its own web address, HTTPS and sign-in. Nothing opens your home network, and your own domain needs just one DNS record.

Recipes your AI can follow

Tested cards for apps you'd otherwise rent: bookmarks, photos, passwords, analytics, private AI and more. A skill teaches your AI to choose one with you and set it up properly.

Looked after, with proof

Every card is tested every night on real machines. Backups are checked by restoring them, and moving to a new machine is built in. A record of who touched your data is next.Coming

Fair questions

You're probably wondering…

Isn't self-hosting for nerds?

It was. Now your AI does the nerdy bits, following recipes that have been tested many times over.

Isn't running apps at home insecure?

Nothing opens your home network: the connection goes out, not in. Every app gets HTTPS and the right sign-in for who should reach it.

What if my computer dies?

Backups are checked by restoring them, and moving to a new machine is built in. That's the spare tyre.

Can't my AI already set up apps for me?

It can, once. We give it tested recipes, then keep things working for years. Setting up is day one; we're there for all the days after.

Doesn't Cloudflare or Tailscale do this?

They do the front door, and they're good at it. That's one part of three: they don't set up your apps, back them up or test them. If you only need a tunnel and you're technical, use theirs. We're for people who want the whole thing looked after.

What if Edgible goes away?

Your apps keep running. They're standard software on your own machine, and only the front door is ours.
